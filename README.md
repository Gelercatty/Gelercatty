# Hi, I'm Yanwen Ma 👋

You can also call me **Geler (格尔)**.

I'm a **CS graduate student at Beihang University**, currently interning with the **[Echo Team](https://github.com/Echo-Team-Joy-Future-Academy-JD) at JD Explore Academy**.

Before diving into generative AI, I spent quite a bit of time with **skeletal animation, motion capture, real-time rendering, and game development**. I still love graphics and games, and I'm always curious about new ways to make virtual worlds more **alive, interactive, and imaginative**.

I also work part-time as a **Technical Artist at [SentNet](https://sentnet.top/)**, mostly working on making things look better :)

## 🌱 Things I'm interested in

- **Computer Graphics** — PBR, shading, rendering, and game engines
- **Generative AI stuff** — video, motion, 3D assets, and worlds
